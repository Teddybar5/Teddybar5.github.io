Animalia is 3D isometric rpg narrative experience in active development.

I am the sole puzzle designer for the project

Puzzle Design

For this project I was tasked with creating 6 unique puzzles that incorporated all major mecahnics in interesting ways, and seemed to fit within the world naturally. This meant no large buttons or levers. It meant that everything should feel like a natural part of the world, and the roadblocks just happen to block the way. The core mechanics were hitting things, and pulling things towards you. I started by making a puzzle solely with hitting things, and forcing the player to think outside the box to solve it, which would teach them that they have to do this in general and allow for more difficult and interesting puzzles. Next I wanted to show that the puzzles interact with the world and the enemies, and they are not entirely seperate. I build upon this idea in the next puzzles where you have to push and pull certain objects and interact with the world in order to cross a river and proceed.
